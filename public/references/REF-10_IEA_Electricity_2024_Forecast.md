# IEA Electricity 2024 - AI Data Center Power Forecast - Reference Archive

## Executive Summary
This report analyzes global electricity demand dynamics, highlighting the intense power requirements of AI data center buildouts and the strategic role of energy companies in providing dispatchable power.

## Key Findings

### 1. Data Center Electricity Demand Surge
- Global data center, AI, and cryptocurrency power demand is projected to double from 460 TWh in 2022 to **1,000+ TWh by 2026**.
- AI cluster racks consume 40–100 kW per rack, compared to 5–10 kW for standard cloud computing racks.

### 2. Bilateral Commercial Synergy (Project Interchange)
- Hyperscalers need firm, dispatchable power (natural gas with CCS, geothermal, nuclear, solar+storage).
- Oil and gas operators possess power generation capacity and land rights, creating opportunities for reciprocal cloud-for-power commercial agreements.

## Citation & Strategy Alignment
Validates Force 1 and forms the strategic foundation for Project Interchange (Appendix D.1).
