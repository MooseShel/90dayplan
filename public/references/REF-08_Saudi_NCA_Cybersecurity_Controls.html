# Saudi National Cybersecurity Authority (NCA) Regulations - Reference Archive

## Executive Summary
The NCA Essential Cybersecurity Controls (ECC) and Communications, Space & Technology Commission (CST) frameworks regulate cloud service providers serving the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia's critical infrastructure.

## Key Compliance Requirements

### 1. Data Classification & In-Country Residency
- **Class C Data**: Sensitive national energy data, subsurface reservoir models, and SCADA control parameters MUST be stored and processed within physical data centers located inside KSA boundaries.
- No cross-border data transfer permitted for restricted critical infrastructure datasets without explicit CST authorization.

### 2. Sovereign Key Management & Zero Trust
- Operators must maintain complete control over cryptographic keys using **External Key Management Services (EKMS)**, preventing cloud provider access to unencrypted payloads.
