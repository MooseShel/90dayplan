# Precedence Research - AI in Oil & Gas Market - Reference Archive

## Executive Summary
This report analyzes AI technology deployment in oil and gas, covering computer vision, generative AI, natural language processing, predictive maintenance algorithms, and subsurface machine learning models.

## Key Market Dynamics

### 1. Market Size & Growth Projections
- **2024 Market Size**: $5.4 Billion.
- **2035 Market Size**: $18.7 Billion.
- **CAGR (2024–2035)**: 13.0%.

### 2. Major Application Areas
- **Subsurface Interpretation & Seismic Analysis**: Generative AI models for fault detection, horizon picking, and automated well log correlation.
- **Predictive Maintenance & Asset Integrity**: AI algorithms processing sensor data to detect compressor failure, pump degradation, and pipeline corrosion before breakdown occur.
- **Refinery Yield Optimization**: Real-time closed-loop control models adjusting temperature and catalytic parameters to maximize high-value yields.
