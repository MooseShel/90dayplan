# IDC Worldwide Industry Cloud Tracker 2025 - Reference Archive

## Executive Summary
IDC's Industry Cloud Tracker models cloud infrastructure, platform, and application adoption across energy enterprise sub-segments. The study highlights high growth in subsurface high-performance computing (HPC), edge IoT telemetry, and sovereign cloud environments.

## Market Segmentation & TAM/SAM Calculation

### 1. Total Addressable Market (TAM): $21.0B by 2028
- **Upstream (E&P)**: $9.8B (Subsurface seismic processing, reservoir simulation, OSDU data platforms, field robotics).
- **Midstream Logistics**: $4.2B (Pipeline SCADA, terminal management, leak detection, logistics routing).
- **Downstream & Refining**: $5.5B (Refinery process optimization, predictive maintenance, retail fuel networks).
- **Decarbonization & CCUS**: $1.5B (Carbon capture tracking, emissions monitoring, regulatory compliance).

### 2. Serviceable Addressable Market (SAM): $9.5B
Google Cloud's differentiated SAM focuses on high-value, high-margin workloads where Gemini AI, OSDU integration, TPU infrastructure, and Mandiant sovereign security provide structural advantage.

### 3. Serviceable Obtainable Market (SOM): $3.2B Target
Target capturing 20% vertical market share by Year 3 by converting key NOCs, Supermajors, and Large Independents.

## Citation & Strategy Alignment
Establishes the TAM ($21B), SAM ($9.5B), and SOM ($3.2B) market sizing in Section 1.3 of the strategy document and the interactive market dashboard.
