# Global CCS Institute - Global Status of CCS Report 2025 - Reference Archive

## Executive Summary
The Global Status of CCS 2025 report documents unprecedented acceleration in CCUS deployment, driven by IRA 45Q tax credits in the US, EU Innovation Fund grants, and NOC net-zero commitments in the Middle East.

## Key Statistical Benchmarks

### 1. Facility Count & Capacity
- **Active Operational Facilities**: 77 commercial-scale projects with total CO2 capture capacity exceeding 55 Mtpa.
- **Under Construction**: 47 facilities adding 35+ Mtpa of capture capacity.
- **Early Development**: 350+ projects in FEED or feasibility stages globally.

### 2. Investment Acceleration
- **Annual Investment Pipeline**: Surpassed $5 Billion annually in 2025.
- **Historical Growth**: Represents a 15-fold increase in annual capital commitments compared to 2020.
- **CAGR**: CCUS project pipeline capacity has expanded at over 30% annually since 2017.

## Citation & Strategy Alignment
Supports Force 5 (Energy Transition & CCUS) and forms the data foundation for the standalone Google Cloud CCUS Strategy document.
