# IEA World Energy Outlook 2025 - Reference Archive

## Executive Summary
The International Energy Agency (IEA) World Energy Outlook 2025 provides global energy projections across multiple policy scenarios (Stated Policies Scenario - STEPS, Announced Pledges Scenario - APS, and Net Zero Emissions by 2050 - NZE). The report highlights that global energy demand continues to evolve alongside accelerated investments in clean energy technology, while traditional oil and gas operators face dual pressures to optimize lifting costs and reduce operational carbon intensity.

## Key Findings & Data Points

### 1. Market Revenue & Scale
- The global oil and gas industry generates **$4.9 trillion in annual revenue**, operating complex upstream assets, midstream transmission pipelines, and downstream refining and petrochemical complexes.
- Despite massive revenue, **public cloud adoption in O&G stands at approximately 18%**, compared to 40–45% across other heavy industrial sectors.

### 2. Capital Expenditure & Decarbonization
- Annual global energy investment surpassed $3 trillion in 2025, with clean energy spending outstripping fossil fuels by a 2-to-1 ratio.
- Oil and gas operators are directing $50B+ annually toward operational decarbonization, methane leak detection, and carbon capture initiatives.

### 3. Upstream Data Isolation
- Major operators maintain legacy data silos spanning 20 to 30 years of subsurface interpretation, seismic surveys, and production logs.
- Over 80% of generated operational data from SCADA and IoT sensors remains unanalyzed or isolated in proprietary field historians.
