# IPCC Special Report on Carbon Dioxide Capture and Storage - Reference Archive

## Executive Summary
The IPCC assessment details the technical feasibility, storage security, economic costs, and environmental monitoring requirements of CCUS technologies.

## Key Scientific Conclusions
- Properly selected and managed geological reservoirs (depleted oil/gas fields and deep saline aquifers) retain **>99% of injected CO2 over 1,000 years**.
- Advanced digital twin modeling and 4D seismic monitoring are mandatory to ensure containment compliance and prevent leakage.

## Citation & Strategy Alignment
Provides scientific backing for the Google Cloud CCUS Think Big Strategy document.
