# Society of Petroleum Engineers (SPE) - JPT Surveys - Reference Archive

## Executive Summary
SPE technical publications and workforce studies analyze demographic trends, digital energy adoption, and knowledge retention across petroleum engineering, geophysics, and reservoir modeling disciplines.

## Core Findings

### 1. Workforce Demographics & Retirement Cliff
- The average age of petroleum geoscientists and reservoir engineers is now **over 50 years**.
- Approximately **35% of senior domain experts are projected to retire by 2030**, risking loss of unwritten basin knowledge and interpretive intuition.

### 2. Data Fragmentation Crisis
- Major operators manage **20 to 30 years of siloed data** across Petrel, Techlog, OpenWorks, OSIsoft PI, paper well logs, and custom SQL databases.
- Engineers spend up to 40% of their working time finding, cleaning, and formatting data rather than performing technical analysis.

## Citation & Strategy Alignment
Validates Force 3 (Workforce Aging Crisis) and Force 2 (Data Fragmentation Crisis) in Section 1.2 of the Google Cloud strategy.
