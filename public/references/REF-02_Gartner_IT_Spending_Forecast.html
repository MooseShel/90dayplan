# Gartner IT Spending Forecast & Cloud Share Q2 2025 - Reference Archive

## Executive Summary
Gartner's energy industry vertical analysis examines enterprise software, IT services, data center systems, and cloud infrastructure spending across global oil and gas operators. The research identifies accelerated demand for AI-driven operational optimization, cloud data platforms, and cybersecurity defense systems.

## Key Findings & Data Points

### 1. Vertical IT Expenditure
- Oil and gas companies spend **$44 billion annually on IT and digital infrastructure**, growing at a compound annual growth rate of 6–8%.
- Cloud IaaS and PaaS spending represents the fastest-growing segment of this expenditure, expanding at >18% YoY.

### 2. Hyperscaler Market Share Breakdown
- **AWS**: 37% market share (strong legacy footprint in upstream data and enterprise ERP).
- **Microsoft Azure**: 32% market share (broad adoption of Office 365, Azure AI, and Schlumberger DELFI partnerships).
- **Google Cloud**: 10% market share (differentiated strength in BigQuery, TPU acceleration, and Mandiant security).
- **Others / On-Prem**: 21% market share.

### 3. Growth Projections
- Gartner projects vertical cloud spending in energy to reach $21B+ by 2028 as operators migrate core OSDU subsurface data platforms and edge operational technology to hyperscaler environments.
