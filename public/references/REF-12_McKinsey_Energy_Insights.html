# McKinsey Energy Insights - Operational ROI - Reference Archive

## Executive Summary
McKinsey's research analyzes capital allocation strategies across IOCs and NOCs, emphasizing targeted digital use cases that deliver margin expansion within 6 months.

## Key Operational Levers
- **NPT Reduction**: Predictive analytics reduce Non-Productive Time on drilling rigs and production platforms by **15–25%**, saving $2M–$5M per deepwater asset annually.
- **Lifting Cost Optimization**: Closed-loop AI models lower energy consumption per barrel lifted by 8–12%.
