# Everest Group - Energy Cloud & GSI Accreditation Analysis - Reference Archive

## Executive Summary
Everest Group's research examines cloud adoption patterns, IT service provider capabilities, and GSI partner ecosystems across oil and gas enterprises globally.

## Key Insights

### 1. Multicloud Reality
- **78% of energy enterprises** have adopted explicit multicloud architectures to avoid vendor lock-in and leverage specialized hyperscaler capabilities.
- Operators frequently run primary ERPs on Azure/AWS while choosing Google Cloud for high-performance BigQuery analytics, TPU training, and Mandiant threat intelligence.

### 2. Strategic Partner Ecosystems
- GSI domain practices (SLB, Accenture, Deloitte, Wipro) dictate up to 45% of enterprise cloud deployment decisions through pre-integrated industry solutions.

## Citation & Strategy Alignment
Validates Section 1.4 and Appendix B.1 regarding Google Cloud's multicloud wedge strategy and GSI alliance program.
